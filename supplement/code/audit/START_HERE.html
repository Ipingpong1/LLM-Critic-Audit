First pass

  1. Enter your initials. Work in your own browser profile. A second reviewer needs a separate profile and CSV; do not exchange judgments yet.
  2. Read the problem and every solution step. A correct final answer does not erase an earlier error.
  3. Choose a verdict below, give the earliest affected step for an error or gap, and record confidence plus a short mathematical reason. If you use a calculator, symbolic tool, or reference, record what you checked.
  4. Use “Can't tell” for unresolved mathematics or damaged text. Describe the obstacle. Do not silently repair the text the critics received.
  5. Export a backup after each session. Finish all 42 items, including explicit “Can't tell” answers, before the first-pass analysis. Do not treat unfinished rows as confirmations of existing labels.

How I can help

After saving and exporting your independent first pass, send an audit ID, your provisional judgment, and the mathematical question. I can check calculations, find counterexamples, or help distinguish an error from a missing justification. Keep each assisted revision separate: original verdict, assistance used, mathematical reason, revised verdict, and your final decision.

If you want help before finishing all items, first export your current independent judgments and identify the assisted items explicitly. Such items must be described as AI-assisted; they cannot count as independent human validation.

After export

Save the CSV in this project's audit/ folder and tell me its filename. I will validate its sheet ID, all 42 item IDs, reviewer identity, and step numbers; preserve a hashed copy; then calculate the two fixed readings of “gap only.” “Can't tell” items are excluded from audit-label estimates and counted explicitly. Unreviewed benchmark items retain their original labels.

With two reviewers, preserve both independent files, report agreement and disagreements, then resolve disagreements in a separate adjudication record. With one reviewer, report a single-author audit; AI checking does not supply a second independent reviewer.

This is a selected diagnostic audit, not a random benchmark sample. It can correct audited labels and test result sensitivity; it cannot establish the benchmark's overall label-error rate. Results based on original labels and audit-adjusted results remain separate versions.
