How to judge
  • The task is the one the critics had: decide whether the solution contains at least one error in any step: a calculation mistake, a logical or conceptual error, or a misreading of the problem.
  • No error: every step is right.
  • Gap only: nothing stated is false, but a step is asserted without enough justification, or is loosely worded.
  • Error: a step states something false, makes an invalid inference, miscalculates or misreads the problem. It counts even when the final answer comes out right.
  • For "gap only" and "error", give the earliest step where it happens.
  • This sheet holds no labels and no model verdicts, and it does not say how many solutions contain an error.
  • Complete all 42 items before the first-pass results are analyzed. You can take breaks and export partial work. This is a selected audit set, not a random sample of the benchmark.
  • Work independently before viewing benchmark labels, model answers, or another reviewer's judgments. Record a short mathematical reason and your confidence. Use "Can't tell" when needed; do not guess.
  • For a difficult item, save your first judgment before requesting AI help. Keep that assistance and any revised judgment in a separate record. Do not overwrite the independent first-pass CSV.
  • Different reviewers must use separate browser profiles and separate CSV files. Enter your initials before starting. A calculator or symbolic check is fine if you record the tool and what it checked in your note.
  • Answers are saved in this browser only. Export the CSV when you stop and keep the file in audit/. Import it to continue in another browser.
  • If the mathematics looks broken, tick "Show unrendered text" to see the characters the critics saw.
